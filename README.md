## 1. Executive Summary
**Homura_Recon** is a specialized network auditing tool developed for Blue Team
operations. Unlike traditional scanners designed for offensive penetration testing,
Homura_Recon is engineered for **Defensive Surface Mapping**. Its primary objective is to
provide security administrators with an immediate and transparent view of the exposed
attack surface of a target host or network segment.
## 2. Operational Objectives
The tool is designed to fulfill three critical defensive requirements:
* **Attack Surface Reduction:** Identify unnecessary open ports that should be closed to
minimize the entry points for attackers.
* **Service Verification:** Ensure that only authorized services (e.g., SSH, HTTPS) are
running and exposed.
* **Baseline Auditing:** Establish a "known-good" state of network services to detect
unauthorized service installations (shadow IT) or backdoor listeners.
## 3. Technical Architecture
### 3.1 Core Engine
The tool utilizes a **Multi-threaded Socket Architecture**, enabling high-speed scanning
without the overhead of heavy external dependencies. By implementing a concurrent
scanning model, it minimizes the time-to-result while maintaining low system resource
consumption.
### 3.2 Scanning Logic
* **TCP Connect Scanning:** Employs a standard TCP three-way handshake to verify port
availability with high reliability.
* **Service Identification:** Performs basic banner grabbing and port-to-service mapping to
identify the nature of the exposed application.
* **Targeting:** Supports single-host auditing and targeted range scanning for subnet
verification.
## 4. Defensive Application (Blue Teaming)
In a professional SOC (Security Operations Center) or incident response scenario,
Homura_Recon is applied as follows:
1. **Post-Incident Audit:** After a suspected breach, it is used to check for newly opened
ports that may indicate a persistence mechanism (Reverse Shells).
2. **Hardening Validation:** After applying firewall rules, it verifies that the ports are indeed
unreachable from the outside.
3. **Compliance Checking:** Regularly audits servers to ensure they adhere to the
organization's "Minimal Services" policy.## 5. Conclusion
Homura_Recon provides the transparency necessary for effective perimeter defense. By
shifting the focus from "finding vulnerabilities" to "managing exposure," it serves as a critical
asset in the Aegis Framework's detection phase.
